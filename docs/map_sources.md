# Cartographic Sources and Usage (Figure 1)

Figure 1 comprises a regional hydrogeological location map (HLM, panel a), a detailed study area map (SAM, panel b), and monitoring well locations (panel c).

## Cartographic Interpretation Boundaries

- WHYMAP serves solely as regional hydrogeological background and should not be interpreted as local aquifer boundaries, shallow/deep aquifer contact lines, or well screen depths. No unverified flow directions, cross-sections, or thicknesses are depicted.
- The 37 deg N line represents an analytical division for study grouping; the six cells centered on 37 deg N are assigned to the southern group. Neither 37 deg N nor the lowland contour represents a physical aquifer boundary.
- Panels b and c show an extent slightly wider than the study domain to present complete 1 deg grid cells; the red rectangle in panel a denotes the study extent (112-120 deg E, 34.5-40.6 deg N).
- Rivers provide generalized geographic reference and do not represent actual streamflow rates, recharge paths, or causal mechanisms during the study period.
- City, mountain, and provincial names serve as geographic labels.
